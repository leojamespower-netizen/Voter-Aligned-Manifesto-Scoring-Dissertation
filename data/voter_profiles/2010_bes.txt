SLOT 8  Instrument notes
No left-right and no authority-liberty item were asked in this wave. The European integration item uses a five-point approval format rather than a graded scale. Respondents are a five-year panel survivor subset, which skews towards the politically engaged in a way demographic weighting does not correct.
