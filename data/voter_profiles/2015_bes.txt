SLOT 8  Instrument notes
Salience categories are the 50-category frame applied to unprompted open-ended responses. No immigration self-placement was asked in this wave; the questions carried instead ask whether immigration benefits the culture and the economy, which are not equivalent. The authority-liberty figure is a derived multi-item scale rather than a single self-placement, so it is not directly comparable to the single-item measures in the other slots.
