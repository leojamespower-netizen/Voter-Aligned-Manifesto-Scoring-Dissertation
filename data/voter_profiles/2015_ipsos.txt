Issue salience, from a monthly national survey of British adults.

Question: what is the most important issue facing Britain, and what other issues are important? Unprompted, so respondents supply their own answers, and multi-response, so one person may name several. Percentages are the share of respondents mentioning each issue and therefore do not sum to 100. All 33 published categories are listed, in the survey's own wording.
